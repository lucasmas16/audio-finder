# Device icons

Nine stylized PNG illustrations, 256 × 256 pixels, RGBA with transparent backgrounds. They were generated with an image-generation tool, then downscaled and reviewed at 96px and 36px on dark and light backgrounds. They contain no text, logos or personal data.

They depict generic device categories, not specific commercial products, and imply no affiliation with or endorsement by any manufacturer.

Shared style: Windows device-picker icon readable at 32px; one centered device; rounded 3D emoji-like illustration, soft bevels, crisp silhouette, front three-quarter view, upper-left studio highlight; subject fills about 85% of the canvas; transparent background; no floor, cast shadow, text, labels, watermarks, borders or extra objects.
